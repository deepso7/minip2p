# NAT machines share session dials to the same peer

An attempt's relay leg, the reservation manager and the AutoNAT prober may all need a session to the same peer (an AutoNAT server can double as the configured relay). Two dials to one peer would make the second connection replace the first and end its streams, so `Shared::pending_session_dials` gates them, and `acquire::start` waits on a connection or a pending dial instead of dialing again:

- **An entry lives for the dialer's full flight deadline**, not the waiter's patience: `Attempt` registers its dial with `relay_timeout_ms` even when the leg's own deadline is nearer, and a probe registers `probe_deadline_ms`. A connection landing after a leg gave up still lifts the gate; a shorter lifetime would reopen the duplicate-dial window while the handshake is under way.
- **Entries are bounded without a timer.** Expired entries only accumulate when handshakes silently die, so `Shared::session_dial` prunes them on each new dial.
- **A failed shared dial wakes its waiters.** When a session dial fails, every attempt sharing it (other than its owner, which learns through its own routing) issues its own dial at once (`on_session_dial_failed`); nothing else re-enters a waiting relay leg, so they would otherwise burn their deadline on a dial that already failed. Housekeeping waiters fall back to their acquire/probe deadlines.
- **A silently stalled owner cannot strand a waiter.** The entry expires exactly at the owner's flight deadline, so a tick runs then, and `Attempt::on_tick` re-drives the relay leg (`redrive_relay_leg`).
- **A dial failure matters only while the leg waits on it.** Once another connection to the relay landed, or the leg moved on, the failure is stale. When an earlier address of the current relay gives up after the leg moved on to an address that was waiting on that dial, the leg dials the new address immediately.
- **An established connection ends the peer's other dials.** While the peer stays connected, every other pending session dial toward it is dropped, since further dials would replace the connection. A buffered establishment the swarm already closed again leaves dials started after that close in flight.
